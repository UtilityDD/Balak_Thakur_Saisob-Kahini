# Audio Drama & Immersive Voice Direction — *পরম বিস্ময় বালক ঠাকুর*

1. **Dialogue-First Rule (65/35 Split):** Every scene opens with a short 1–2 sentence atmospheric narrator setup (`Enzo`), then immediately hands the stage to live character conversations (`2–5` rapid exchanges per scene).
2. **Emotional Sub-Text in Every Style Box:** Never write generic styles like `"Normal voice."` Always describe the **internal emotion + shift** (e.g., `"Starts skeptical and mocking, catches breath in sudden shock, softens into hushed awe."`).
3. **Natural Prosody Tags (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`):**
   - `<short pause>` for dramatic silence, realizations, and natural breathing.
   - `<laugh>` for warm chuckles, childhood mischief, or ecstatic mystic joy.
   - `<whisper>` for secret doubts, conspiracies, or sacred midnight hush.
4. **Two-Speaker Dialog Blocks:** Use paired speakers for fast banter (parents whispering in amazement, schoolboys teasing, skeptic vs. devotee) so timing feels alive.
5. **Soundscape Integration:** Every scene pairs voice acting with `ambient_bed` (Padma river, Dhaka bazaar, Chandranath hills wind, monsoon rain), `music_cue`, and beat-synced `sfx`.
