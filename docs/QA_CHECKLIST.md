# QA Checklist: Episode Frame Review

Use this checklist to review every episode the same way, from a contact sheet of rendered stills (plus a few short clips where motion must be judged).
Every check comes from `01_Series_Bible/DIRECTOR_DECISIONS.md` or `docs/PIPELINE_DECISIONS.md`; the rule ID is shown at the end of each line.

## How to review

- Reviewers only report; they never edit files (QA-04).
- Copy this checklist into the review report for the episode. Tick each check that passes, and mark a check N/A when the episode has no such shot.
- For each failure, give the frame number (24 fps), the scene, the rule ID and one line on what is wrong.
- Motion checks (marked *motion*) cannot be judged from one still: compare consecutive stills, or ask for a short clip of that range.
- When a rule is added or superseded in either decisions file, update this checklist in the same session.

## 1. Balak: likeness and costume

- [ ] No tilak or tikka on Balak's forehead (BAL-01)
- [ ] No bangles or anklets on Balak (BAL-02)
- [ ] No jewellery and no sacred thread on Balak (BAL-03)
- [ ] Balak's hair is curly and dark (BAL-04)
- [ ] Age matches the episode: newborn or infant in Episode 2, crawling infant of about 8 to 10 months in the go-saap scene, about five in Episode 3 (slight visual difference from the narration is acceptable) (BAL-15, BAL-16)
- [ ] Five-year-old: white or cream cotton dhoti with an uttariya across the chest and over one shoulder (BAL-17)
- [ ] Five-year-old's dhoti and uttariya follow the pose in every frame (BAL-18) *motion*
- [ ] Infant: loose cotton langot with a visible knot, hanging end and folds, not a hard solid band (BAL-19, BAL-20)
- [ ] Infant's langot trails the hips by a few frames (BAL-20) *motion*

## 2. Balak: divine light and face

- [ ] Divine light appears on Balak only; no other character, animal, prop or effect has it (BAL-05)
- [ ] Halo is a soft warm-white radial glow behind his head, fading out at its edge (BAL-06)
- [ ] No hard rings, dashed rings or bursting rays (BAL-07)
- [ ] Warm golden rim light on his hair, shoulders and back (BAL-08)
- [ ] Faint glow on the ground under him (BAL-09)
- [ ] Only a few slow rising motes, never a shower (BAL-10)
- [ ] The light brightens gently only on key beats (smile, miracle reveal) and is otherwise steady (BAL-11) *motion*
- [ ] Face serene and kind; soft half-lidded bright eyes with catchlights (BAL-12)
- [ ] Faint gentle smile; soft cheeks with a blush (BAL-13)
- [ ] Slower blinks than other characters; peaceful stillness (BAL-14) *motion*

## 3. Other characters and costume

- [ ] Charu reads as mid-20s (CHR-01)
- [ ] Charu wears a white sari with a red border, and sindoor (CHR-02)
- [ ] Married women show a thin red alta line on their soles (CHR-03)
- [ ] Thakurma wears white and has no alta (CHR-04)
- [ ] Walking women's feet show below the sari (CHR-05)
- [ ] Walk: planted foot fixed, swing foot heel first, hem lifts over the forward foot (CHR-06) *motion*
- [ ] Sari hem and red border curve and fold with the cloth, never a straight line (CHR-07)

## 4. Body and motion

- [ ] A held baby never floats; the holder's hands stay on him in every frame (MOT-01)
- [ ] Cradle hold: one forearm under his bottom and thighs, the other hand on his back or behind his head (MOT-02)
- [ ] A lift begins only after the hands are under the baby (MOT-03) *motion*
- [ ] The holder bends or kneels to reach the baby (MOT-04)
- [ ] Lifts are slow and gentle (MOT-05) *motion*
- [ ] Feet stay planted; no sliding (MOT-06) *motion*
- [ ] Contact kept: hands on the ground stay down, held objects stay in hand (MOT-07)
- [ ] Adults show idle life: weight shift, eyes leading glances, soft-elbow arm sway, small fidgets, cloth lag (MOT-08) *motion*
- [ ] Idle life is reduced during deliberate poses (MOT-09) *motion*
- [ ] No snapping between poses; changes are eased (MOT-10) *motion*
- [ ] Overlapping action: eyes before head, upper arm before forearm, cloth trailing (MOT-11) *motion*
- [ ] Anticipation dip before big moves, settle after (MOT-12) *motion*
- [ ] No frozen pose longer than about 20 frames unless the line calls for it (MOT-13) *motion*
- [ ] No stiff outstretched arms (MOT-14)
- [ ] Gestures start 6 to 8 frames before their narration cue and return to rest about 20 frames after (MOT-15) *motion*
- [ ] Crawling infant: diagonal pairs, trunk roll, bottom wiggle, sliding knees, flat hand slaps, uneven rhythm with pauses, head lifted, soles up (MOT-16) *motion*
- [ ] Crawling infant: chubby infant proportions, three-quarter view (MOT-17)
- [ ] Crawling infant's torso is not perfectly level or animal-like (MOT-18)

## 5. Camera, staging and transitions

- [ ] Slow push-ins on key beats and pull-backs to breathe (CAM-01) *motion*
- [ ] Rack focus and parallax layers are in use (CAM-02)
- [ ] Handheld movement only in tense moments, and light (CAM-03) *motion*
- [ ] Letterbox bars only on sacred moments (CAM-04)
- [ ] Big events get a short slow-motion beat (CAM-05) *motion*
- [ ] Key lines get a face close-up of about 1.3 to 1.5 on Balak or Charu, about 18 frames in, held through the line, about 20 frames out (CAM-06) *motion*
- [ ] Faces and key action are above y = 820 (clear of the caption band) (CAM-07)
- [ ] No background edge is visible during any zoom or pan (CAM-08)
- [ ] Related scenes join with a true cross-dissolve of about 36 to 48 frames, not a quick fade through black (CAM-09) *motion*
- [ ] Separate stories are divided by a chapter break: fade to black, short hold, soft chime, slow fade in on a calm establishing view (CAM-10) *motion*

## 6. Story fidelity and sets

- [ ] Every scene shows what the narration says, literally (STO-01)
- [ ] The Vedic house is the existing `BaidikBariDay` set (STO-02)
- [ ] The inner courtyard is the existing `CourtyardBack` and `CourtyardFloor` (STO-03)
- [ ] No known location is redrawn (STO-04)
- [ ] Episode 2 only: the rain-blocked Shashthi puja, the move to Ujanchar after the job news, the fox through the far wall, the rice pot covering the smiling baby, and the dense jungle around Ujanchar all match STO-05 to STO-09

## 7. Animals

- [ ] Animals are realistic and readable (ANI-01)
- [ ] Bagha is a strong tan village dog with no stripes (ANI-02)
- [ ] The fox's dark lower legs have a warm rim light and read against a dark wall (ANI-03)
- [ ] The cow is a humped Bengal zebu (ANI-04)
- [ ] The go-saap is a big Bengal monitor with frequent tongue flicks (ANI-05) *motion*
- [ ] The go-saap feels frightening while the baby plays fearlessly (ANI-06)

## 8. Titles, captions and cards

- [ ] All on-screen text is in Baloo Da 2 (TTL-01)
- [ ] Intro uses the Episode 1 layout: eyebrow, title, gold pill with the episode label, episode name animated word by word (TTL-02, TTL-04)
- [ ] No narration captions on the intro title page (TTL-03)
- [ ] Source card follows the title: centred book-page card, graceful motion (TTL-05)
- [ ] Source card: book name on its own line, memoir credit on its own line, English lines below (TTL-06)
- [ ] Subtitles: soft dark gradient band, cream text of about 44 px with a soft shadow, short slide-up (TTL-07)
- [ ] No chip for the narrator; gold chip only for named characters (TTL-08)
- [ ] Outro: the sunset with the series title and narration captions (TTL-09)
- [ ] Outro preview window: centred, about 620 x 350, thin gold border, calm divine moment from the next episode, readable label beneath (TTL-10, TTL-11)
- [ ] No empty end hold after the outro (TTL-12)
- [ ] No place-name title board where the narration already says the name (TTL-13)

## 9. Audio (listen to the clip, or check with the render agent)

- [ ] Narration is clear over every sound effect and loop (AUD-01)
- [ ] Sound-effect volumes are 0.15 to 0.45; loops are 0.25 or below (AUD-02, AUD-03)
- [ ] Animal voices and the baby's cry are real recordings where available (AUD-04, SFX-01)
- [ ] Voices measure -16 LUFS integrated with a true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP (PAU-01)
- [ ] Every recording used is listed with source, author and licence in the sound folder's README, with a credit line for the video description (SFX-02 to SFX-04)

## 10. Process sign-off (before a full render)

- [ ] Stills and contact sheets were rendered and looked at for every change (QA-02, QA-03)
- [ ] Type-check passes (AGT-04)
- [ ] Protected fixes from earlier sessions are still in place (AGT-02)
- [ ] The director has seen and approved the frames (QA-01)

## 11. Final render verification (after the director approves)

- [ ] Rendered in parallel chunks of about 2,000 frames and joined with ffmpeg concat (REN-01, REN-02)
- [ ] Joined file has the expected duration (REN-03)
- [ ] No blank frames at any join (REN-04)
- [ ] Copied to `03_Episodes/<Episode_folder>/04_Video_Assembly/Final_Render/<Episode_folder>.mp4` (REN-05, LOC-03)
