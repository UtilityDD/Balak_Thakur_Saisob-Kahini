# Generalized Intro & Sequential Outro Guide

Every episode (`Episode_01` through `Episode_19`) shares a unified broadcast structure:

1. **Series Intro (`00_Intro.wav`):**
   - **Shared Visual Bumper:** `00a_series_bumper` (Padma sunrise + lotus/saffron glow + `Theme_Intro_Bansuri.wav`).
   - **Dynamic Episode Title Card:** Spoken by `01_Narrator_Kathak`:
     > `ছোটদের বালক ঠাকুরের গল্প। <short pause> পর্ব {পর্ব সংখ্যা}: {পর্বের নাম}।`

2. **Sequential Outro (`99_Outro_Sequential.wav`):**
   - **Part 1 (Moral Reflection):** Warm 1–2 sentence takeaway tailored to the episode's spiritual/human lesson.
   - **Part 2 (Sequential Next-Episode Hook):** Points directly to `Episode N+1` by title and dramatic hook:
     > `ছোটদের বালক ঠাকুরের গল্প—আসছে পরের পর্ব: {পরের পর্বের নাম}! {আকর্ষণীয় ইঙ্গিত}`
   - **Season Finale (`Episode 19`):** Concludes the 3-season childhood arc with a serene series-closing blessing.
