# Episode 6 immersive re-cut: plan (pilot of STO-22)

**Status: APPROVED 2026-11-23 (STO-22a)** on the recommended answers to section 6 (the director: "proceed"). Build in progress. The delivered cut
(2026-11-23, 2,978 frames) stays in `Final_Render` until the re-cut is approved, rendered and checked; it is then backed
up outside the repo before being replaced.

Rule: **STO-22, faithful story, cinematic telling** (`01_Series_Bible/DIRECTOR_DECISIONS.md`). Everything in the
approved Episode 6 plan (`Ep06_Shots_and_Pauses_DRAFT.md`, STO-21) still stands: every narrated event is shown, the
voices, recorded lines, places and people do not change. This plan only **adds** wordless moments around them, and
re-stages a few shots for point of view and movement.

How to read the tables: **[B]** = taken from the book's unnarrated details (chapter 10, row 16; chapter 12, row 18);
**[V]** = everyday village life (our staging, nothing the book contradicts). *New time* is time added to the episode
(inserts, action gaps or longer pauses through the timing generator); *inside* means it fits in existing time.

  lengthened (G2). Re-generate `timing.ts` and `subtitles.ts`.
- **Set** (`UjancharVillageField.tsx`): a **village lane** view (a path between hut fences, a haystack by the path, the
  friend's house at its end), usable for W1 (a) too; a **fallen log** at the field's edge; a sunset light for G2 (or
  the afternoon light pushed lower and warmer).
- **Village life** (new file, e.g. `src/cinematic/life/VillageLife.tsx`): hen with chicks (walk, peck, scatter), crow
  (perched, take-off), sparrow (hop, peck), goat (tethered, chewing, bleat head-lift), dog (off screen only, so none),
  paper kite on a string (tug and sway), a woman with a brass water pot (walk-through, a CAST adult with the pot).
  No glow on any of them (BAL-05).
- **Scenes** (`Scene01SavingElderBrother.tsx`, `Scene02TreasurerAndTenRupees.tsx`): the added shots, inserts, point
  of view, tracks and afterglows above, keyed to the new gaps and pauses; new events exported for sound.
- **Sound** (`Episode06.tsx`) and **cue sheet**: the added beats (pastoral world opens, the heartbeat in C1, the comic
  plucks in D1 and L1, the afterglows); new SFX sourced as CC recordings: hens clucking, crow caw and wing flap,
  sparrow chirp, goat bleat, dog bark, a slate tapped and a plate clink, a soft heartbeat (or a slowed drum stroke
  from the music library).
- **Unchanged:** the rigs, the cast, the props, the outro and its Episode 7 glimpse.
