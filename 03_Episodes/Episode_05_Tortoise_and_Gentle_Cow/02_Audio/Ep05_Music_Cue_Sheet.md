# Episode 5: cinematic music and sound cue sheet (2026-11-22)

The director asked for cinematic sound and music that follows each scene and situation (AUD-07). Gentle, devotional,
Bengali-village colour: tanpura, bansuri, santoor/sitar, tabla/dholak, soft drums. Children's series: tension is light
and never frightening. Rules kept: the narration stays clear (AUD-01); music ducks under every voice and rises in the
pauses (AUD-06); loops at 0.25 or below (AUD-03), one-shots 0.15 to 0.45 (AUD-02); voices at -16 LUFS.

Frames are absolute Episode05 frames (scene 01 starts 639, scene 02 starts 1254); cue by the scene exports
(`S01_TIMES`, `S02_TIMES`) and narration cues, not hard numbers, where possible.
