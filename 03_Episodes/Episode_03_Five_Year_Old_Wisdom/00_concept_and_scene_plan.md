# Episode 3: পাঁচ বছরের মুখে অমূল্য তত্ত্ব (Wisdom of a Five-Year-Old)

Production concept and scene plan. Runtime about 92 s (SRT ends 00:01:32.205). 1920x1080, 24 fps. Times are from the SRT unless marked "est.", which are estimated from character share within a cue.

## 1. Summary and core lesson

Five-year-old Balak Thakur (Biru in the narration) pours water at the tulsi platform, places hibiscus on his own feet and bows to himself. Charu scolds him that he will sin and God will curse him. He answers that a living god would feel the flowers, that clay idols neither move nor understand, and that if he were God he would not be angry even if trees were cut. The narrator closes with the teaching and a teaser for Episode 4.

Core lesson, plain words: God lives in love, truth and goodness, not in fear or showy ritual. Kindness to living things matters more than ritual rules.

### S01 Dawn garden (cue 2, 7.50–22.18 s)
- **Narration:** "শরতের মিষ্টি ভোর। শিউলি ফুলের গন্ধে আকাশ-বাতাস ভরে উঠেছে। বাড়ির মেয়েরা ভোরবেলা স্নান সেরে জবা আর ঝুমকো ফুলের সাজি ভরছে।" A sweet autumn dawn. The air is full of shiuli scent. The women of the house, bathed at dawn, fill baskets with hibiscus and jhumko flowers.

- **SFX / ambient / music:** `river_water`; `dawn_birds_temple`; `Village_Peace_Loop`.
- **Subtitle breaks:** after "ভোর।" at about 9.6 s and "উঠেছে।" at about 14.1 s (est.). Possible split: S01a (air and river pan) and S01b (basket action).
- **Emotion:** calm, sensory.

### S02A Flower act (cue 3, 22.63–36.07 s)
- **Narration:** "হঠাৎ সবাই দেখে পাঁচ বছরের ছোট্ট বীরু তুলসীতলায় জল ঢেলে ঝরাপাতা আর ডালা থেকে ফুল তুলে নিজের পায়ে দিচ্ছে, আর নিজেই নিজেকে প্রণাম করছে!" Suddenly everyone sees little five-year-old Biru pouring water at the tulsi platform, taking fallen leaves and flowers from a basket, placing them on his own feet, and bowing to himself!
- **Camera:** slow dolly push-in 1.00x to 1.12x, sine in-out, anchored to Balak's eye-line. Camera at his eye level, never above.
- **Blocking, in order:** (1) kneels at tulsi-tala and pours from a small clay kolsi onto the roots; (2) reaches to a flat dala for hibiscus; (3) bends forward and lays flowers and leaves on each foot, toes flexing; (4) joins hands and bows from the waist, holds about 1 s, rises slowly.
- **Props:** tulsi-tala, kolsi, dala, fallen leaves on the terracotta floor.
- **SFX:** water-pour and leaf-rustle foley (new; not in manifest). Keep `soft_magical_shimmer` for 02C.
- **Subtitle break:** after "দিচ্ছে," at about 33 s (est.), splitting placing from bowing.
- **Emotion:** wonder and gentle humour. The act reads as play, not ritual.

### S02B Charu's reaction (cue 4, 36.52–47.08 s)
- **Narration (Charu):** "ওমা, একি কাণ্ড! তুই ঠাকুরের ফুল নিজের পায়ে দিচ্ছিস কেন রে? দেখিস তোর পাপ হবে, ভগবান শাপ দেবেন!" Oh my, what is this! Why are you putting the god's flowers on your own feet? Mind you, you will sin, and God will curse you!
- **Camera:** locked-off medium on Charu at eye level; 0.4 s push to 1.04x on "শাপ দেবেন!".
- **Blocking:** Charu enters from left. Hand to mouth on "ওমা". Steps back half a pace on "একি কাণ্ড!". Veil slips and she pushes it back. Points toward Balak on "তুই ... রে?". Two girls at right turn heads in a staggered 2–3 frame beat. Balak stays at tulsi-tala and does not turn.
- **SFX:** day-birds ambient (new). No shimmer.
- **Subtitle break:** after "একি কাণ্ড!" at about 37.7 s (est.).
- **Emotion:** alarm, half scold, half tease.

### S02C Balak's reply (cue 5, 47.53–63.44 s)
- **Narration:** "তোদের ভগবান তোদের শাপ দেবে! আমার পায়ে ফুল দিলে তো আমি বেশ টের পাই। তোমাদের মাটির মূর্তি তো নড়েও না, কিছু বোঝেও না! আমি ভগবান হলে কিন্তু ফুল কেন, গাছ কেটে নিলেও কাউকে রাগ করতাম না!" - **Subtitle breaks:** after "শাপ দেবে!" (about 49.7 s), "টের পাই।" (about 53.3 s), "বোঝেও না!" (about 57.4 s), all est.

## 5. Animation notes

1. **Kneel and place (02A)** is the key gesture. Weight shifts through the knees, toes flex as flowers land, and one flower slips and is set back.
2. **The bow (02A):** hands join, head and shoulders lower together, hold about 1 s, rise on a slow ease.
3. **Stillness on Balak:** breathing at 0.25 Hz; blink every 3–4.5 s, 120 ms closing; no eye darts, even while pouring.
4. **Charu is the contrast:** quick reactions with hand and veil.
5. **Hands and cloth:** fingers wrap around stems and the kolsi handle; dhoti pleats and anchal sway 1–2 frames late.
6. **Animals:** none in the script. A sparrow on the tulsi-tala in 02C would show nature settling around him. Director's call.
